📖 Learn GEX Edge

Dealer Delta Hedging:

Options trades create directional risk. When you buy a call option, a market maker sells it to you, leaving them short exposure. To hedge, they buy shares of stock. As stock price moves, they continuously adjust this hedge, creating real buying/selling pressure on the S&P 500.
