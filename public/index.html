📖 Voltick Level Dictionary

★ Volt

The big one, the strongest options level. Usually set from overnight positioning.

⚡ Surge

Today's active hotspot where new options volume and capital is stacking up right now.

◆ Coil

A major positioning cluster before the Volt. Green slows price, red speeds it through.

⚡ The Flip

The mood line. Above it is calm positive gamma; below it is wild negative gamma.
